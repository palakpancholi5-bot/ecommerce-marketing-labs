# E-Commerce Performance Marketing Labs

Practical datasets and audit frameworks used in live classroom exercises.

## Lab 1: E-Commerce Campaign ROAS & Funnel Audit (`roas-audit-lab.csv`)
Students analyze multi-channel campaign data across Google Search, Google Shopping, and Meta Ads to calculate:
- **CTR & CPC:** Evaluating ad relevance and traffic efficiency.
- **Conversion Rate (CVR) & CPA:** Identifying landing page or audience bottlenecks.
- **Actual ROAS vs. Target ROAS:** Making budget reallocation decisions (scaling retargeting vs. fixing broad prospecting).
